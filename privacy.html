📋 About This Policy

JIBU ("we", "us", "our") is a WhatsApp and SMS-based learning platform that helps Tanzania secondary school students prepare for NECTA examinations. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

📂 What Data We Collect

When you register with JIBU we collect only the information needed to run the service:

  • Full name — so we can personalise your questions and reports
  • Phone number — to send you daily exam questions via WhatsApp or SMS
  • Form level and subjects — to generate relevant NECTA-style questions
  • Parent or guardian phone number — to send optional weekly progress reports
  • Quiz answers and scores — to track your progress and identify weak areas

We do not collect payment card details. Payments are processed through M-Pesa; we only store the M-Pesa confirmation reference you provide to us.

🎓 How We Use Your Data

Your information is used solely to deliver the JIBU learning experience:

  • Sending you daily NECTA-style exam questions and feedback
  • Tracking your streak, points, and ranking progress
  • Generating weekly performance summaries for you and your parent or guardian
  • Sending Saturday bonus quiz challenges
  • Sending service notifications such as trial expiry and payment reminders

🔒 We Do Not Sell Your Data

JIBU does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party, ever.

We share data only with the infrastructure services required to operate JIBU — specifically Airtable (data storage), Africa's Talking (SMS delivery), and the WhatsApp Business API (WhatsApp delivery). Each of these providers is contractually bound to protect your data and use it only to fulfil our service.

🗄️ Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored in Airtable on servers located in the United States. We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information against unauthorised access, loss, or disclosure. No internet transmission is 100% secure, but we take your privacy seriously and work continuously to improve our safeguards.

🧹 Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service. If you would like your data deleted, please contact us at the email address below and we will remove your record within 14 days.

👤 Your Rights

  • Request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Request correction of any inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your account and personal data
  • Opt out of the service at any time by contacting us
